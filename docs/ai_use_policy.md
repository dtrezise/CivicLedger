# AI Use Policy

## Allowed Uses

AI and deterministic automation may:

- discover and retrieve public sources under their published access rules;
- extract text and candidate fields while retaining page and source evidence;
- normalize names, dates, assets, and identifiers with reversible provenance;
- identify duplicates, amendments, missing fields, and records needing review;
- rank possible event context using documented, neutral criteria;
- draft summaries that remain linked to source records and explicit evidence states;
- assist with software, tests, accessibility, documentation, and release operations.

## Prohibited Uses

AI may not:

- fabricate a filing, source, quote, reviewer, review decision, or outreach response;
- infer intent, privileged knowledge, corruption, ethics violations, insider trading, or illegality;
- promote L2 parser output to L3 reviewed or L4 publication-ready status;
- present synthetic regression benchmarks as empirical investigative accuracy;
- treat temporal proximity, office, committee, or jurisdiction as proof of relevance;
- suppress conflicting evidence or hide extraction uncertainty;
- publish private research notes, credentials, contact information, or unpublished media.

## Required Disclosure

Public records must expose whether fields are source-observed, normalized, machine-extracted, or human-reviewed. AI-generated prose used in editorial products requires source verification and human editorial responsibility. The accountable publisher, not the model, owns every public claim.
