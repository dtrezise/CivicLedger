# CivicLedger Roadmap

Updated October 2026. CivicLedger is a provenance-first federal financial-disclosure research system spanning the Obama administration to the present. The public explorer is useful for discovery and comparison, but it remains a research preview because no transaction has completed attributable human review and judicial transaction acquisition has not begun.

## Current State

- 2,169 tracked officials and 5,925 dated roles across all three federal branches.
- 75,786 source-linked timeline transaction candidates: 54,457 House, 14,164 Senate, and 7,182 presidential OGE preview rows.
- 4,427 sourced events, 223,452 official-event relationships, market and crypto history, source snapshots, and neutral event-ranking support.
- Zero reviewed public-production trades and zero judicial disclosure documents.
- A public static explorer, a FastAPI/Next.js review application, source-specific ingestion lanes, OCR evidence, immutable review events, and release gates.
- A daily updater with weekly deep-source work, last-known-good publishing, and public refresh health.

## Phase 1 - Restore and Measure Reliability

- Keep the daily updater green and publish last-known-good status on upstream failure.
- Complete a representative disclosure gold corpus and event-relevance review set.
- Publish empirical parser and ranking metrics separately from synthetic regression tests.
- Add source-lane service objectives, retry budgets, and failure ownership.

## Phase 2 - Human Verification Operations

- Staff and calibrate the reviewer workflow.
- Review the first representative House, Senate, and executive batches.
- Enforce dual review for ambiguous identity, amendment, and high-impact records.
- Promote only records meeting L3, then complete L4 editorial checks for public narrative use.

## Phase 3 - Coverage Completion

- Finish House image/OCR and legacy transaction extraction.
- Expand Senate acquisition and amendment review.
- Acquire executive OGE disclosures beyond presidents under applicable request rules.
- Register for and acquire judicial financial disclosures, preserving access restrictions.
- Add declared coverage denominators for every source, office, year, and document type.

## Phase 4 - Public Product Quality

- Make evidence state, freshness, missingness, and source provenance visible in every view.
- Add accessible table and narrative alternatives for each chart.
- Improve official comparison, event windows, source inspection, and correction notices.
- Validate desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Phase 5 - Editorial and Community Launch

- Name an accountable editor and legal/privacy review process.
- Publish a monitored corrections channel, privacy policy, terms, and right-of-reply procedure.
- Develop citation-preserving formats for articles, podcasts, video, newsletters, and local outreach.
- Establish membership and community verification programs without paywalling source evidence or corrections.

## Historical Expansion

Expand earlier than 2009 source by source, not administration by administration. Publicly useful coverage depends on filing availability and format. Build a source-era map, acquire stable official archives, validate representative samples, and publish gaps. Reagan-era ambitions are appropriate for roles and selected official records, but transaction-level comparability will be uneven and must never be implied where disclosure regimes or digital archives differ.

## Release Blockers

- No human-reviewed public transaction corpus.
- No judicial disclosure acquisition.
- No empirical parser-accuracy or event-ranking validation.
- No public correction intake or named editorial ownership.
- Previously exposed credentials require rotation before production trust.
