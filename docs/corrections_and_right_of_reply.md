# Corrections and Right of Reply

## Correction Record

Each material correction must record the affected entity, prior value, corrected value, source evidence, reason, reviewer, decision time, dataset version, and whether public narrative material changed. Corrections are append-only; regenerated datasets must retain a reproducible audit trail.

## Intake and Triage

Before editorial launch, CivicLedger must publish a monitored correction channel and response target. Requests should be acknowledged promptly, triaged by potential harm and factual significance, and resolved against primary evidence. Credible safety, identity, or materially false-record reports receive priority.

## Right of Reply

Before publishing a consequential finding about a person or institution, provide the specific factual proposition, supporting records, reasonable response deadline, and a way to supply documentary evidence. Publish material responses and unresolved disagreements fairly. Silence is not evidence that a claim is true.

## Public Notice

Material public corrections should identify what changed and when without repeating unnecessary harmful language. Cosmetic changes may be logged internally. Retractions require a visible public notice and preserved internal evidence.
