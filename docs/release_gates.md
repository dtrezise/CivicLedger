# Release Gates

## Automated Gates

- Source files and generated partitions parse successfully and match their schemas.
- Hashes, counts, shards, and public manifests reconcile.
- Preview, reviewed, production, fixture, and missing states remain distinct.
- Backend tests, frontend build, rendered interaction tests, accessibility checks, link checks, performance budgets, and deterministic builds pass.
- No tracked live credentials or prohibited private research material are present.
- The daily updater records public health and keeps the last validated snapshot on failure.

## Human Gates

- L3 record promotion requires an attributable reviewer and source comparison.
- L4 publication requires verification editing, provenance review, fairness review, and correction readiness.
- Consequential reporting requires right of reply and review of contradictory evidence.
- Public launch requires a monitored correction channel, named editorial owner, privacy policy, terms, and incident response contact.

## Stop Conditions

Do not publish new generated data when a gate fails, source coverage falls below its declared floor, amendment reconciliation becomes destructive, evidence-state labels disappear, or a credential/private record may have entered an artifact. Preserve diagnostics privately and serve the last validated public snapshot with a freshness warning.
